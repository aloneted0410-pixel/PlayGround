The rulebook

Think in three dimensions.

Two armies. One cube. Your pieces can travel through all eight layers—not just across a board.

♜
Rooks & queens

Slide along straight lines through any of the cube’s three axes. Bishops also travel along 2D and 3D diagonals.

♘
Knight & king

Knights leap in 3D L-shapes. Kings step one cell in any direction. Keep yours safe.

♙
Pawns

White advances toward higher layers; Black toward lower ones. Capture diagonally in the next layer. Reach the far face to promote.

⌗
Explore the cube

Use the layer strip or arrow keys to inspect each 8×8 slice. Click a glowing square to complete a move across layers.

Standard check rules apply. Pawns promote to queens. Castling is not used in Voxel Chess.
